Culture & traditions

Discover the traditions that make Madurai special.

A LIVING HERITAGE

Tradition in every street

Madurai's identity is shaped by Tamil language, devotion, craftsmanship, music, dance and community celebrations. Its streets remain active from early morning to late night.

Visitors can experience local markets, traditional arts, temple festivals and the warmth of everyday city life.
